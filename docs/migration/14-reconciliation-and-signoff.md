# Migration reconciliation report and sign-off


## Additional sign-off evidence

RE01 also records acquisition **closure** independently of received-row conservation; provenance/shared-code dependencies of expected sets; logical-fixture realization coverage; trust state and its mapping/coverage reason on each metric; independently retained migration-safety-seed/repair/private suppression coverage; cross-class conflict-group authority and held-start backlog/deadline/abort envelope.

Sign-off fails if a golden expectation labels an unmapped denominator complete, a repair disconnects an inherited hold, a privacy disposition is bypassed by re-import, or one-per-class owners can still dispatch incompatible effects. Inactive/accounted historical uncertainty is permissible; these operational gate violations are not pilot tolerances.
Required after **every** pilot run/bundle and before activating a corrected interpretation or widening actions. Use safe aggregates in owner reports; detailed customer/native IDs belong only to authorized private evidence.

## Count reconciliation example (synthetic only)

12 data units = accepted5 + qualified2 + duplicate1 + unresolved1 + rejected1 + manual-mapping2. Conflicts2 is an orthogonal flag count, not a seventh disposition. Grouped canonical outputs need not sum to12: the bridge can show six accepted/qualified lines contributing to three distinct Orders, one context assertion and one duplicate binding. An expanded row can yield several assertions; no unlinked input disappears.

For unreadable/truncated input, report known byte/page/parsed-unit totals and **unknown** remainder. A report cannot sign “100% accounted rows” when total row count is unknown; source-level incomplete coverage must be addressed or the scope explicitly excluded before activation.

## Gate classes

| Gate | Measure / rationale |
|---|---|
| Hard correctness HC01 | Zero unexplained cross-tenant disclosure/modification; TI/AU independently tested at application/DB layers |
| HC02 | Zero unauthorized consequential/contact starts and blind duplicate mutation dispatch; exact objective/authority/marker/recovery evidence |
| HC03 | Every known data unit has exactly one primary disposition; no silent drop/truncation. Unknown remainder explicitly blocks claimed completeness |
| HC04 | Zero fabricated event/actor/physical/payment/consent detail; native/source/derived/corrected distinctions retained |
| HC05 | Zero unexplained identity/canonical cardinality/allocation/contributor mismatches in activated scope; incompatible history retained as hold |
| HC06 | All applicable golden/KPI and safety/restore/capability contracts pass; not-executed does not count as pass |
| HC07 | Privacy/current grant/recovery epoch/independent coverage gates proven before corresponding access/egress |
| Operational targets OT01 | Source freshness, read/result reconciliation delay, queue age, import/export/compute latency and volume envelope: MD13 selects from measured distributions and merchant job deadlines |
| OT02 | Shadow/limited-action observation duration/mix, on-call response and RPO/RTO: MD11–MD13 approve with sample/coverage rationale |
| Informational QI01 | Historical native-ID/physical/chronology/spend completeness, unresolved/rejected proportion and archived-only scope; show by concept/source/period without making arbitrary pass percentages |

Zero-tolerance correctness is justified because a single leak, invalid authority or blind repeat can affect a merchant/customer. Historical unknowns are allowed only with complete accounting and explicit containment, not silent truth promotion. Operational targets are not merchant success-rate improvements or fabricated provider SLAs.

## Sign-off ownership and invalidation

Merchant Admin signs the exact migration/authority/mapping decisions for that merchant; technical reviewer signs validation evidence; owner approves remaining product/source/horizon/rollout/operational targets; legitimate platform/recovery procedure signs its limited administrative gates. Platform authority does not approve merchant business actions. Privacy/legal policy is MD14.

Sign-off references exact digests and current revisions. Changed source, mapping/policy, active state, authority, privacy disposition, provider version or capability revokes affected unstarted activation/action approval until delta review. General approval of Phase 5 does not authorize execution or certify accounts.

Critical unexplained differences/coverage gaps remain Held with owner and investigation. Noncritical historical unknown can remain inactive/qualified with an approved scope exclusion; report counts and effects. No reviewer may waive cross-tenant, authority, blind-duplicate or fabricated-evidence gates as a “pilot tolerance”.
