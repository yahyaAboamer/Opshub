## Deferred SaaS product-boundary decisions

The owner-requested NEW SAAS correction establishes the distinction between User identity, E03 Merchant Membership and E26 Platform Operator Assignment. This distinction is not another unresolved business-policy entry, and none of PD01–PD25 is reopened or resolved by it. Native account matching and reconstructed merchant-side questions in PD11/PD20 remain as recorded.

Exact platform roles/permissions, super-admin capabilities, support impersonation, delegated access and access-control implementation are later PRD/architecture decisions. Provisioning, invitation, suspension, merchant onboarding, deletion and support-access workflows are deferred to the PRD; this register introduces no workflow or authorization engine.

SaaS commercial/billing concepts are outside the reconstructed Gentry operational domain and will be evaluated during PRD/product-boundary work. No Subscription, Plan, Invoice or SaaS Payment entities are added; their omission does not decide that the SaaS will have no billing.

## Decision record required for closure

For each decision record: owner/authorized decision-maker, chosen semantics, source/evidence distinction, effective date/version, affected entities/projections, historical treatment and remaining evidence limitations. This is a domain approval record format, not an application workflow. All rows currently have **no recorded owner resolution**.

Approve identity/grain separately from numerical policy. An approved future definition can differ from Gentry, but must be labeled a new/normalized canonical decision; it must not rewrite Phase 1 or claim retrospective adoption.
