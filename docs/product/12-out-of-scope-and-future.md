## Unsupported inputs must remain visible

Encountering an unsupported source condition does not authorize this scope automatically. Preserve original observations and reason; hold uncertain classification/action, show impact, and obtain a bounded human decision. Multiple historical Gentry legs can include reverse purposes, and must not be treated indiscriminately as multiple outbound shipments. Repeated or partial episodes cannot be silently merged into OD06's single case.

The MVP will not reconstruct contact/delivery events from counters, assert paid pickup collection, manufacture finance receipts or reproduce absent historical inputs. Core connections, safe fallback and Product/SKU/ROAS trust are now MVP requirements, not deferred quality. Exact unsupported provider operations stay named fallback exceptions; a known unknown must not become an automatic execution promise.
