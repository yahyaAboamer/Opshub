## Target authority

Historical Config C44:E53 has owner-target labels for RTO .10/.15, customer returns .05/.08, attempts 2/2.5, cause capture .90/.75, reach .70/.55, recovery .25/.15, cycle 6/8 days, queue age2/3 days, unmapped cities/unpriced SKU0. They are internal targets, not industry standards.

Config C223:C225 explicitly flags confirmation .60/intervention .15/pickup no-show .30 as proposed `[CONFIRM]`. Current report also presents provisional confirmation/intervention lines, and its pickup/courier targets need precise denominator confirmation. Product settings explicitly ask Hassan to confirm defaults. The owner must distinguish currently configured values from approved policy.

## Human instructions versus executable rules

The SOP's48h return work, weekly refresh, month-close cleanup and quarterly taxonomy review are operational obligations. Nothing inspected proves an automatic scheduler enforces them. Call resolution/refund text is recorded information, not demonstrated financial execution. Side effects of reship/cancel/archive remain unverified; do not fill those gaps with industry conventions.
