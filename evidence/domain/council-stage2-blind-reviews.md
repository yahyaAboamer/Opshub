# Blind peer reviews — first complete draft critiques

Three fresh reviewers evaluated the same anonymized responses in [Stage 1](council-stage1-anonymized.md), on accuracy and insight. Reviews concern the first complete draft, before the corrections recorded in [quality review](quality-review.md). No peer saw the original members' identities/lenses. Below are their returned evaluations and rankings.

## Peer 1

**Response A:** Most convincing overall. Its wallet finding is verified: Phase 1 identifies a cash-cycle row, while E24 and the mapping emphasize charge/fee grain. The retained profile confirms all six cited monetary/payment fields are populated across 1,381 rows and 78 cash-out IDs exist. Its report-review finding also identifies a real semantic omission: BR24, L26 and reconstruction 04 distinguish Operations approval from data-quality eligibility, but the canonical state-machine document does not preserve that boundary explicitly. A correctly avoids claiming enforced approval or financial settlement. The classification suggestion is useful clarification, although taxonomy/source distinctions already exist. Allocation simplification is appropriately labeled optional; stable attribution-result identity can be justified by traceability without requiring an additional operational lifecycle.

**Response B:** Strong and narrowly actionable. Its wallet finding is accurate. The retry criticism identifies genuinely risky wording in E15: unchanged intent does not exclude several externally evidenced consequences. However, I18 already separates intent, retry observations and attributable results, and other documents acknowledge unknown duplicate effects; this is a local precision defect rather than wholesale loss of duplicate semantics. Separating native-object namespace from evidence lineage is insightful. E04 currently includes both account/store namespaces and workbook lineages, so an engineer could incorrectly make each export lineage define a distinct external object. B’s proposed conceptual distinction addresses that risk without prescribing architecture.

**Response C:** Adds valuable evidence, especially the populated Business ID observation. The profile confirms one distinct Business ID across 1,381 rows. Preserving it as a binding candidate is warranted. Calling this an established source-account identity defect is stronger than the evidence allows: a field labeled Business ID does not establish a native courier-account namespace. C acknowledges that limitation, which keeps its proposed correction safe.

C’s intake and revision concerns are less persuasive as omissions. E10 explicitly recognizes summary observations; identity document 02 says to retain intake summary observations and native row locators; document 03 separates cumulative counters from events. Naming the complete summary bundle would improve precision, but the model already gives it a conceptual home. E18 explicitly distinguishes logical series identity from revision evidence identity. Clarification may still help E22/E23, but claiming inconsistent treatment across all three overstates the gap. Its consequence-multiplicity proposal is sound and overlaps B.

**Supported corrective synthesis:** Preserve wallet-row cash-cycle assertions and source cash-out references separately from assessed fees, with no settlement inference or additive double counting. Preserve the Business ID observation while leaving its boundary meaning unresolved. Add the report eligibility-versus-human-review distinction as historical intent/current unexecuted wording. Clarify native-object namespace versus capture lineage, and revise E15 so repeated observations alone establish neither another consequence nor the absence of duplicate consequences. Explicitly name intake-summary and calculation-revision contracts where useful, treating these as precision improvements rather than evidence-supported new entities. Retain unresolved episode identities, provisional links and conditional entities.

FINAL RANKING:
1. Response A
2. Response B
3. Response C

## Peer 2

**Response A:** Strongest overall. Its wallet finding is directly supported by reconstruction 07 and the retained profile: all 1,381 rows contain the named monetary/payment fields, with 78 distinct Cash-out IDs. Its report-review finding identifies a separate business boundary omitted from domain 04: reconstruction 04 and BR24 distinguish data-quality eligibility from Operations approval before forwarding. Preserving review provenance alone does not explain that distinction. Classification clarification is useful, although domain 03 already separates narrative, courier exceptions, governed causes and responsibility. Treat that recommendation as tightening an existing contract. The allocation-entity concern is appropriately presented as optional simplification, not a proven defect.

**Response B:** Accurate and insightful. The wallet correction is sound. Its retry wording concern targets an actual ambiguity in E15; I18 already permits separately attributable effect results, so the necessary correction is principally consistency rather than a new business rule. Multiple unintended results should be permitted as a conceptual possibility, without asserting that Gentry actually produces them. The namespace-versus-artifact distinction is particularly valuable for migration traceability. E04 already places file revisions within a lineage, which reduces ordinary export-version splitting, but still combines provider identity scope and evidence lineage. Clarifying their different roles would improve the multi-merchant model.

**Response C:** Supplies useful additional evidence but overstates some deficiencies. The profile confirms a populated Business ID column with one distinct value; retaining it as a binding candidate is justified. However, that does not refute the narrower statement that native account/store identity remains unknown: its provider-boundary semantics are still unverified. Call this a missing identifier observation, rather than proof that account uncertainty is overstated. The intake concern also overlooks domain 02’s explicit “Intake record” summary-observation contract, reinforced by domain 03. Naming its full field bundle would help, but the summary concept is already present. Likewise, E18 expressly distinguishes logical series identity from revision evidence identity. E22/E23 could use greater consistency, but the claimed general omission is too broad. Its effect-multiplicity recommendation is sound.

**Corrective synthesis:** Preserve wallet rows at their source cash-cycle grain, name COD/online/net/payment/time assertions and Cash-out grouping, and distinguish components from totals to prevent double counting. Retain Business ID as an observed identifier with unresolved boundary meaning. Add the separate report-review boundary with historical-intent/current-UI labels and revision-specific approval assertions; do not invent enforced sending behavior. Distinguish provider object namespaces from artifact provenance. Reword E15 so retries or receipts alone do not establish additional consequences, while independently evidenced duplicate results remain representable. Strengthen existing intake-summary and revision contracts where needed without adding mandatory entities, settlement lifecycles or unsupported cardinalities.

## Peer 3

**Response A:** Accurate and restrained. The wallet finding is verified: reconstruction 07 identifies cash-cycle grain, while E24 and the projection table describe charges. Its report-review finding is also supported by BR24 and reconstruction 04: data-quality eligibility and Operations approval are separate boundaries, and canonical 04 omits the latter. The classification suggestion is useful but a proposed clarification; the draft already separates narrative, exception, cause and responsibility. Treating allocation identity as optional is reasonable, although independently traceable calculation revisions can legitimately have identity without a separate operational lifecycle. “Approved report revision” must remain proposed; persistence was not verified.

**Response B:** All three concerns are sound. E15’s assertion that repeated attempts are not new consequences is too categorical when duplicate execution remains unknown. I18 already distinguishes intent and attributable effect results, so the necessary correction is principally local wording and explicit multiplicity. Separating native object namespace from artifact lineage is especially valuable for migration traceability. E04 already places individual file revisions within a lineage, which limits the concern; B correctly identifies the remaining distinction between a lineage and the underlying account namespace. It misses the independently supported report-review and Business ID coverage issues.

**Response C:** Offers the broadest supported corrective insight. The retained profile confirms Business ID in 1,381 rows with one distinct value, alongside the wallet fields and 78 cash-out IDs. Its recommendation to preserve a binding candidate without declaring Merchant/account equivalence is correct. However, calling this a source-account identity defect slightly overstates what the field proves: source-reported business identity is available, while account-boundary semantics remain unresolved.

C’s intake concern also needs narrowing. E10, identity rules and the event/projection table already explicitly preserve intake summaries and prohibit converting counters into events. A named bundled summary classification would improve precision, but there is no established omission of the grain itself. Its series/revision concern is useful for E22/E23; E18 already supplies logical series identity and separate revision identity, so this is consistency work rather than a missing distinction. Its effect-multiplicity recommendation agrees with B and is warranted.

**Supported corrective synthesis:** Preserve wallet/cash-cycle observations, named payment assertions and source cash-out grouping separately from assessed charges, without asserting settlement. Retain Business ID as observed evidence with unresolved namespace semantics. Add the evidenced Operations-review boundary separately from quality eligibility, distinguishing stated contracts from executed transitions. Clarify intent, execution evidence and zero/one/multiple externally evidenced results; repeated receipts may describe the same result. Separate native object namespaces from artifact provenance. Tighten intake-summary, classification and revision descriptions only where they improve explicit semantic contracts; avoid additional mandatory entities or invented lifecycles.

The draft remains a strong review proposal. These corrections should not resolve provisional return/pickup identities, provider uniqueness, customer matching or financial execution through commerce conventions.

FINAL RANKING:
1. Response C
2. Response A
3. Response B
