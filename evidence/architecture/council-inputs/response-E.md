I recommend **conditional approval of the architecture**, after the two recovery-contract corrections below. The direction is faithful and suitable for a small team; it is not yet an implementation-ready safety claim. Keep A01–A08 as real qualification/release gates. This recommendation does not authorize a build or reopen OD01–OD09.

The strongest choices are the modular monolith, one relational transaction system, separate web/worker processes, managed authentication and no additional cache/warehouse/service fleet. The proposal preserves business and courier truth, case-only completion, cardinality, Admin authority, provenance and nonadditive product populations. Tenant keys, composite relationships, RLS and checked request/job context form useful complementary defenses. It also correctly acknowledges that platform pool separation cannot contain complete process compromise.

External-action handling is unusually careful: immutable intent, durable objective reservation, one precommitted dispatch, independent effects/results, unknown blocking both connected and manual execution, and no automatic compensation. Public provider contracts are distinguished from account qualification. These safeguards should survive simplification.

**Assumptions:** the team can review TypeScript and explicit PostgreSQL transactions/grants; the named pilot accounts can demonstrate a useful supported round trip and reliable result identification; owners approve source-authority, analytics, privacy and commercial-continuity policies; someone owns unknown-result investigation and recovery. None of those operational capabilities is verified by architecture documentation alone.

1. **P1 — The recovery journal cannot yet reconstruct every lost reservation.** In [13-deployment-and-environments.md](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/13-deployment-and-environments.md), “Protecting external effects across backup rollback,” markers contain local objective/intent/effect IDs, hashes and connection/correlation references. If an objective was created after the restored backup, its local IDs and hash do not identify the original business subject, effect kind or reservation scope. Consequently “rebuild missing objective reservations” is an aspiration rather than a complete recovery contract. A conservative global block prevents immediate duplicates but offers no defined route to safely resume unrelated work. Specify a minimal protected recovery descriptor: verified source namespace/native subject references, effect kind, stable business reservation scope, dependencies and descriptor version, without customer payloads. Define enumeration completeness and reconstruction/hold rules. Extend [14-testing-strategy.md](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/14-testing-strategy.md) with a restore preceding objective creation, including an unresolved lost-response effect. Do not turn the journal into full event sourcing.

2. **P1 — Completed privacy redactions may be forgotten by rollback.** [11-audit-provenance-and-privacy.md](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/11-audit-provenance-and-privacy.md), “Provider data requests and redaction,” and [13](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/13-deployment-and-environments.md), “Backup and restore,” require deletion-manifest replay before reopening. They do not specify where manifests survive independently of the restored database. A deletion completed after the restore point can otherwise disappear with its manifest, resurrecting data despite a successful replay of the older manifests. Define independently retained, access-controlled minimal disposition records, their retention/coverage proof and the access barrier until replay completes. Test deletion after backup followed by restore. This resolves a recovery mechanism; exact legal scope and retention remain A05 owner/legal decisions.

3. **P2 — Revenue attribution needs a clearer contract than spend allocation.** [09-analytics-architecture.md](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/09-analytics-architecture.md), “Canonical ROAS,” requires attributed commerce revenue, while [04](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/04-data-architecture.md) explicitly models spend mappings/allocations and revenue adjustments. An item-to-product link plus ad-to-product spend mapping does not by itself establish revenue eligibility for K07. State separately how the approved revenue attribution method selects contributors, handles unattributed revenue and preserves basis/version/evidence. Reuse contributor records where sufficient. Include an analytical fixture with attributed and unattributed sales of the same SKU; absent an approved rule, qualify/unavailable the numerator rather than silently using all sales. OP09 chooses the rule; engineering must not infer it.

4. **P2 — Bound the physical implementation of the provenance/module design.** [02](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/02-architecture-style-and-modules.md) has eleven modules; [04](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/04-data-architecture.md)/[11](D:/Desktop/Opshub/docs/architecture/11-audit-provenance-and-privacy.md) combine typed state, assertions, interpretations, revisions and projections. These meanings are justified, but implementing every logical record as a separate repository/framework would overwhelm the team and multiply coordination mistakes. Make the stated permission to combine closely related physical records concrete: fixed business-specific interpretation contracts, one checked unit-of-work mechanism, one outbox protocol, bounded retained generations and explicit ownership of each current value. Preserve safety-critical identities and evidence; prohibit a generic policy/EAV/CQRS engine. The selected stack is reasonable, but toolchain/grant compatibility and operating costs remain qualifications, not demonstrated advantages.

The strongest objection to approval is that a broad integrated MVP plus this safety/provenance burden may exceed the team's capacity, while unresolved provider outcomes can immobilize daily work. My response is conditional: architecture approval is sensible, but pilot release must fail if controlled qualification cannot demonstrate recoverable essential actions and staffed exception handling. Documentation quality cannot substitute for those proofs.
