No material issue remains from the four original findings in the amended documentation. Recommend approval of the Phase 4 architecture direction, subject to its existing owner-policy, compatibility and provider/recovery qualification gates. This judgment concerns documentation sufficiency; no application, provider, queue-adapter or restore tests were executed.

1. **Restored authority and privacy — addressed.** Chapter 03, “Service state and emergency continuity,” and chapter 13, “Restored authority and privacy access gate,” now require an independently enforced recovery epoch, invalidated restored sessions/invitations, provisional memberships/platform assignments and verified current grant/service/connection authority. Fresh OIDC identity cannot recertify restored grants, and restored Admins cannot authorize their own recovery. Reads/exports remain closed pending authority verification and independently retained privacy-manifest replay. Chapters 11/13 specify staged privacy dispositions, protected target identities, coverage across restorable backups and inaccessible data when coverage is missing. ADR009 reflects the same boundary.

2. **Constructable recovery reservations — addressed.** Chapter 13, “Protecting external effects across backup rollback,” now records operation/business reservation scope, stable native account/subject bindings, relationships, known results and versioned protocol metadata. Dispatch, manual handover and contact-start descriptors are durable before issuance. Complete paginated enumeration, independent coverage/checkpoint manifests, restore-horizon retention and unresolved holds are explicit. Unrecoverable identity or coverage quarantines the affected merchant/connection rather than inventing a narrower safe reservation. Chapter 06 also retains exclusive pending handover state across storage I/O and blocks both paths after uncertainty. Descriptor presence remains possible execution, never fabricated approval/success.

3. **Pre-call concurrency and evidence — addressed.** Chapters 04/07/08 specify atomic eligibility/actor/ordinal reservation, independent contact descriptor, then current-authority/reservation recheck and issued state before permitting the external call. Storage I/O is outside DB locks. Expiry/reassignment/lost responses cannot restore uncertain allowance; known-unused release requires attributable policy review. Actual call time/reach is separately recorded, and independently made or excess calls remain violating evidence. The architecture correctly limits its guarantee to OpsHub-authorized workflow rather than claiming physical control over external calling.

4. **Canonical case denominators — addressed.** Chapter 04 defines one canonical case per merchant/Order/case_kind and one customer-contact objective per case. Reopening or physical-purpose relabeling cannot mint another identity/budget. Chapter 09 explicitly uses that case for K05/K06 contributors, excludes ambiguous imports diagnostically and does not count reservations as actual contact. Chapter 14 supplies corresponding adversarial fixtures.

The job simplification is sound as a documented contract: chapter 07 and ADR007 require enqueue on the exact domain transaction/connection, rollback together, limited grants, stable domain obligations and safe recovery sweeps. Started/unknown writes cannot be reauthorized by redelivery. Actual adapter enlistment/grant compatibility remains A08 evidence, not a proven capability here.

Chapter 09 separately versions revenue eligibility/attributed amount/unattributed balance and spend allocation; product membership does not attribute revenue. OP09 still selects the method, recognition and basis. Chapter 14’s 15,000/5,000 example explicitly assumes approval and is a test oracle, not invented policy. Recovery feasibility, provider matching, retention policy and adversarial test execution remain mandatory release qualifications.
